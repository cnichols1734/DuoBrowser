# DuoBrowser app icon

One restrained compass: ocean blue background, ivory ring and cardinal marks, vermilion/ivory needle. The familiar compass silhouette signals browsing; the simplified full-blue tile gives DuoBrowser its own identity. No lettering or baked-in corner mask.

Created with the built-in image-generation tool on October 27, 2026. The opaque square original was exported at 1024 × 1024 using macOS sips for the iOS universal AppIcon asset. Xcode supplies required app-icon renditions and the system supplies the corner mask. This is a raster asset, not a layered Icon Composer document; custom dark/tinted variants are not included.

Asset: `DuoBrowser/Assets.xcassets/AppIcon.appiconset/DuoBrowser-Icon.png`. The AppIcon catalog is included in the target resources and selected in both Debug and Release build settings.

## Generation prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: final iOS app icon for DuoBrowser. Create one exceptionally refined, minimal compass browser icon, instantly legible at small home-screen sizes. Square 1024 x 1024 opaque full-bleed artwork, straight-on, no mockup. A uniform deep ocean blue field fills the entire square edge to edge. Center a single precise ivory circular compass ring with four understated cardinal index marks inside it. Inside the ring, one long slender geometric compass needle pointing northeast at 35 degrees from vertical: warm vermilion forward half and ivory rear half, meeting cleanly at the center, no hub ornament. Perfect optical balance, generous negative space, crisp vector-like contours, flat solid colors, meticulous geometric craft. The ring diameter is about 68% of canvas width; a confidently visible medium-weight ring, needle contained within ring. Familiar browser/compass visual language like Safari, but original simplified geometry and deep blue full-tile background, clearly a distinct app. No text, no letters, no globe grid, no extra symbols, no sparkle, no AI motifs, no gradients, no glow, no glass bevels, no shadows, no texture, no 3D, no fine tick-mark clutter, no rounded-square mask baked into image, no outer margin, no presentation board. The result should look like restrained professional identity design for a serious native browser.

## Verification

Simulator build and asset catalog compilation passed. The compiled Info.plist selects AppIcon. The source is an opaque 1024 × 1024 PNG. Installed on DuoBrowser QA and visually verified on its closed Home Screen next to Safari: the ring, four indices and two-tone needle remain distinct at normal icon size. No browser logic changed.
