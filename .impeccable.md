## Design Context

### Users

iPhone Duo owners browsing everyday sites, especially on the closed outer screen. The initial owner wants Safari-level usability with substantially less wasted display area, and smooth animated page adjustments around the selfie camera.

### Brand Personality

Proposed: precise, calm, fluid. The owner approved this direction together with floating bottom controls, camera-avoidance animation, and optional constrained on-device intelligence. This is not final branding. DuoBrowser is a working name.

### Aesthetic Direction

Content dominates. Propose compact floating browser controls and a bottom address field that expands for editing. Support system light/dark appearance and native accessibility. The browser should feel familiar; the distinctive behavior is how it gives space back to the page. The direction is approved; refine exact placement through the first prototype.

### Design Principles

1. Measure usable content and reachable actions, not just painted pixels.
2. Keep essential controls discoverable when browser chrome collapses.
3. Move the minimum necessary page content, keeping meaning and behavior intact.
4. Use motion to explain displacement and restoration; respect Reduce Motion.
5. Keep the same navigation model across outer and inner displays.

This context is grounded in the initial request. The owner approved the brief's direction on October 26, 2026. Exact branding, colors, and animation timings remain implementation decisions to validate.

### Owner refinements — October 26, 2026

Use the owner's Safari screenshots as the reference for native Liquid Glass, a centered domain capsule, separate Back/Tabs circles, and a familiar thumbnail tab overview. Center controls on the entire display rather than the camera-safe content width. When scrolling forward, the capsule moves downward and shrinks to a domain-only pill just above the bottom edge. Side buttons disappear near their outer positions; reverse scrolling or tapping the pill smoothly restores them. Honor the system glass appearance and Reduce Motion.

### Owner refinement — October 28, 2026

Keep website text stable during scrolling, including when it passes behind the camera or Dynamic Island. After scrolling stops, fit text sideways, inward from the camera edge. The owner explicitly rejected pushing text down or adding a gap above it. Keep the original DOM and font size. A full-width paragraph may rewrap once after the pause to fit beside the obstruction; never animate its width or scale its glyphs. If there is no useful space beside an expanded island, keep the original layout. Interruption must hold the current presentation without a jump. Reduce Motion and VoiceOver take precedence. Review with an actual synthetic Live Activity in the Duo simulator as well as fixed geometry fixtures.
