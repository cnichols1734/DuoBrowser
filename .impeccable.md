## Design Context

### Users

iPhone Duo owners browsing everyday sites, especially on the closed outer screen. The initial owner wants Safari-level usability with substantially less wasted display area, and smooth animated page adjustments around the selfie camera.

### Brand Personality

Proposed: precise, calm, fluid. These are interpretations of the owner's Safari reference and request for excellent animation, not approved branding. DuoBrowser is a working name.

### Aesthetic Direction

Content dominates. Propose compact floating browser controls and a bottom address field that expands for editing. Support system light/dark appearance and native accessibility. The browser should feel familiar; the distinctive behavior is how it gives space back to the page. Final control placement remains subject to the first prototype and owner feedback.

### Design Principles

1. Measure usable content and reachable actions, not just painted pixels.
2. Keep essential controls discoverable when browser chrome collapses.
3. Move the minimum necessary page content, keeping meaning and behavior intact.
4. Use motion to explain displacement and restoration; respect Reduce Motion.
5. Keep the same navigation model across outer and inner displays.

This context is grounded in the initial request. The detailed brief remains a draft. Do not interpret proposed controls, names, colors, or timings as final owner decisions.
