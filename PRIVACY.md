# DuoBrowser Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026. Developer: Christopher Nichols. Contact: chrisnichols17@gmail.com.

DuoBrowser is a native web browser. It has no app analytics, advertising SDKs, app tracking service, account requirement or developer-operated browsing backend. We do not receive your browsing history, cookies, passwords, page content or wallpaper through an app telemetry service. No page content is sent to an AI service.

## Data on your device

Normal tabs, bookmarks, history, website settings and app preferences are stored in the app container. WebKit manages normal website cookies, local storage and caches. A wallpaper photo you explicitly choose is resized and saved locally; DuoBrowser does not request access to your entire photo library. App data may be included in operating-system backups.

Delete history and bookmarks through **Page options → Bookmarks & History**. Clearing history does not remove cookies or caches. To remove all normal website data, delete DuoBrowser in iOS Settings. Remove wallpaper through **Tabs → Tab options → Tab Appearance**. Deleting the app removes its local container; separately exported files and operating-system backups are controlled through their respective apps/system settings.

## Private browsing

Private tabs share a separate temporary WebKit data store. Their history, tab URLs, previews and website preferences are excluded from normal archives. Closing all private tabs clears that private session; private tabs are not restored after a cold restart. Bookmarks you deliberately save and downloads you export to Files remain until you delete them. Private browsing does not anonymize traffic or prevent websites, network operators or your internet provider from observing network requests.

## Requests to other services

Websites you visit receive ordinary browsing requests and may collect information under their own policies. Searches go to Google. Plain keywords typed in normal tabs are sent to Google to retrieve search suggestions; website-address input, private-tab typing and unfinished keyboard composition are excluded from these typing suggestions. These requests include ordinary network information such as your IP address. Google may retain or associate requests according to its [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Normal start-page shortcut icons are requested from a site's public home page and declared icon/CDN hosts, without the saved page's path, query or fragment, and without shared cookies or credentials. Icon requests reveal ordinary network information such as IP address to those hosts. The bounded image cache is memory-only. Private start pages do not load these shortcuts.

Offline articles are saved only when you turn on **Save Article Offline** and choose Save. They contain readable text and optional bounded copies of same-origin HTTPS images, fetched without cookies or credentials. Reading a saved article makes no website requests. Saved pages—including explicit saves from private tabs—remain in Library until deleted; remove an offline copy from the Reading List item menu or delete the entry. They may be included in operating-system backups.

## Permissions and files

Websites may request camera, microphone or location access through system prompts. You control permissions in iOS Settings and website prompts. Files/photos selected for website upload go to the destination you choose. Normal downloads and their filenames, sizes and completion dates are retained locally until you remove them in Page options → Downloads. Download files are excluded from system backups. Original download URLs, request headers and WebKit resume data are not saved in the download manifest. Private downloads are temporary and are removed when all private tabs close or on the next app launch; copies you deliberately export to Files are managed by you. DuoBrowser does not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

## Support and beta diagnostics

If you contact support, we receive the information you choose to send, use it to respond and investigate, and retain it only as needed for support or legal obligations. Do not send passwords, cookies, private browsing history or other unnecessary sensitive data. You may contact us to request deletion of support correspondence, subject to legal obligations.

Apple operates TestFlight and may provide crash reports, device information and feedback under its [Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). TestFlight feedback you choose to submit may include screenshots; review them for personal information before sending.

## Changes

Changes will be published here with an updated effective date. The in-app Privacy Policy remains available from Page options.

## Filter updates

When automatic filter updates are enabled, DuoBrowser periodically downloads public EasyList, EasyPrivacy and optional Cookie List source data from easylist.to and secure.fanboy.co.nz. These servers receive ordinary network connection information, but no visited URLs, page content, browser cookies or browsing reports. The optional Cookie List is downloaded with the catalog even when its filtering is off. Filter Lists provides automatic-update controls and offline license credits. Public filter caches do not contain browsing data.
